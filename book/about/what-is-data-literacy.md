# What is Data Literacy?

*Data literacy* refers to how competent an individual is in collecting, analysing, interpreting, 
evaluating, storing, sharing, and communicating data, while bearing ethical, integrity, 
privacy, and security considerations. 

As you might have already noticed, there are multiple competences and aspects to consider in the journey to become data literate. You will require *knowledge* (e.g., the know what), *skills* (e.g., the know how), but also suitable *attitudes* (e.g., embracing specific personal and/or organisational values).

**A reseacher with the required knowledge and skills might still neglect the proper development of a *Data Management Plan* (DMP) in their project, if they have not yet embraced the importance of *FAIR principles*.**

### What about *information* literacy and *digital* literacy?

Nowadays, a lot of the same academic skills apply when working with data and information, whether physical or digital. Navigating this complex landscape quickly becomes challeging for both beginners and experts. Becoming data literate demands development in [related literacies](../basics/related-literacies-overlap.md).

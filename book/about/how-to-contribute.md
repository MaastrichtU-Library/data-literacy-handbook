## I want to contribute

We welcome any feedback, suggestions or input that could help us improve this handbook. You can contribute in one of the following ways:

- Join the discussion in our <a href="https://maastrichtu-library.github.io/data-literacy-handbook/">GitHub repository</a> to share your ideas, report any bugs, or to directly open an Issue. You can take these actions by clicking on the GitHub logo on the top right of every page.
- Contact us by e-mail via ub-dataliteracy@maastrichtuniversity.nl. Let us know where and how you would like to contribute. We are happy to receive your input in Word or other formats, which we can then code into the handbook ourselves.
- If you work at Maastricht University or another Dutch university, we are happy to meet you in person or online to discuss collaboration opportunities.

% note: add later the Data Literacy page, as a link when mentioning potential UM collaborators

We currently do not have any hardcore contribution guidelines. If you are interested in contributing by providing new or revised content, we are happy to receive your input in whichever format or method is easy for you. You will then be listed as a "contributor". In this case, it is important that you agree to the use of your content in this handbook and its licensing.

## External resources
This handbook also aims to serve as a curated collection of (open educational) resources. Therefore, we would like to highlight and generally describe how some key resources have been reused, integrated, and/or adapted in this book. To trace back specific elements within the chapters, please consult the [References](../book/references.md) list.

- <a href="https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3332807">Illustrations from The Turing Way</a> by *Scriberia*: You will find several of their illustrations throughout our chapters. 
- <a href="https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15576175">Research Data Management Guidebook for Students & User-Guide</a> by Li et al. (2026): The content of the guidebook has been almost completely integrated throughout the chapters of the present book, particularly under the *Planning* chapters.

# Welcome to the **Data Literacy Handbook**!

**This handbook is a living document, part of Maastricht University Library’s <a href="https://library.maastrichtuniversity.nl/teach/academic-skills/data-literacy/" target="_blank">Data Literacy program</a>. In a nutshell, this work is a curated collection of (open educational) resources for students and educators.**

If the handbook is completely new to you, we  recommend you to first explore the _About_ section, where we introduce the core concept of [data literacy](../book/about/what-is-data-literacy.md) and also provide a few [usage recommendations](../book/about/how-to-use-this-handbook.md). You are also warmly invited to [contribute](../book/about/how-to-contribute.md)!

In this handbook you will find: 

**1.** Introductions to key concepts and fundamental aspects of Data Literacy  
**2.** Exercises to practice your data literacy knowledge and skills  
**3.** Templates and advice on how to embed data literacy training in education (assignments, courses, etc.)  

The handbook aims to be useful for both students and educators. To get started, we highly recommend you to use our [navigation guide](../book/about/how-to-use-this-handbook.md), where we outline various possible learning trajectories. Of course, feel also free to explore this work in your own way and at your own pace.

We hope you enjoy the handbook, and wish you a lot of success in becoming [data literate](../book/about/what-is-data-literacy.md)!

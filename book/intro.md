# Welcome to the Data Literacy Handbook

**This handbook is a living document, part of Maastricht University Library’s Data Literacy program. Explore the About section to find out more about the handbook and its [recommended use](../book/about/how-to-use-this-handbook.md). You are warmly invited to [contribute](../book/about/how-to-contribute.md)!**

The Data Literacy Handbook is a curated collection of (open educational) resources for students and teachers. In this handbook you will find: 

**1.** An introduction to key concepts and aspects of Data Literacy  
**2.** Exercises to practice your data literacy skills  
**3.** Templates and advice on how to embed data literacy training in education (assignments, courses, etc.)  

The handbook aims to be useful for both students and educators. To get started, we highly recommend you to use our [navigation guide](../book/about/how-to-use-this-handbook.md), where we recommend specific learning trajectories. Alternatively, feel free to explore this book in your own way and at your own pace.

We hope you enjoy this handbook, and wish you a lot of success in becoming [data literate](../book/about/what-is-data-literacy.md)!

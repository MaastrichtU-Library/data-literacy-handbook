# Welcome to the Data Literacy Handbook

**This handbook is a living document, part of Maastricht University Library’s Data Literacy program. Explore the About section to know more about the handbook and its development. You are warmly invited to contribute!**

The Data Literacy Handbook collects knowledge and resources for students and teachers. In this handbook you will find: 

**1.** An introduction to key concepts and aspects of Data Literacy
**2.** Exercises to practice one's data literacy skills
**3.** Templates and advice on how to integrate data literacy skills in education (assignments, courses, etc.) 

The handbook is meant to be useful for both students and educators. To get started, we highly recommend you to use our navigation guide, where we recommend specific learning trajectories. Alternatively, feel free to explore this book in your own way and at your own pace.
